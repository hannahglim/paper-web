Hi, I’m Hannah. I’m an artist and designer. I recently moved back home to Washington in May, with many papers, books, and postcards that I’ve collected over the years and around the world. I’m still not sure how to display on my walls. So, I thought it could live on a screen first.

The page updates globally, so you can see others interacting with the page and moving images in real time (but don’t worry, I can’t see who you are).

I’m interested in thinking about the World Wide Web as a canvas, as well as a public space of shared embodiment, mediated through the screen. I like to think of the internet still as a fun place to hang, and small personal websites are a good place to start (check out Spencer Chang’s alive internet theory).
